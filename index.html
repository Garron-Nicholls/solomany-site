What it does for you

Plans around what you have

The days you can train, the time you can give it, and the equipment you can actually get to that week.

Leads the session

Set by set on your wrist, so you are not reading a screen between efforts.

Watches the limits you set

Heart rate against ceilings you choose. It says something when you cross one; it does not decide for you.

Keeps the thread

What you told it in week two still counts in week nine. You should never have to repeat yourself.

Pick the voice that works on you

Motivation is personal. The training underneath is the same; the coach delivering it is yours to choose, and you can change your mind whenever you like.
